Notes (added afterwards):
- An earlier run with the first version of the script reported a 169 MB/s write; that was the
  random-number generator (os.urandom is slow on macOS), not the disk.
- Random 512KB/2MB reads at 13-22 GB/s are higher than the cold sequential read (6.6 GB/s) and the
  sequential write (7.4 GB/s), so they are most likely helped by caching. Plan with ~6.5 GB/s.
